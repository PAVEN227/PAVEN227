## Featured projects

### 📊 [Netflix Global Top 10: Streaming Trends Analysis](https://paven227.github.io/STA9750-2025-FALL/mp01.html)
Cleaned and analyzed Netflix's global and country-level Top 10 datasets spanning **94 countries**, answering 10 stakeholder questions on content longevity, regional performance, and viewership concentration, with press-release-style summaries for non-technical audiences.
`R` · `tidyverse` · `Quarto` · `Data Cleaning` · `EDA`

### 🏘️ [Housing Affordability & YIMBY Policy Analysis](https://paven227.github.io/STA9750-2025-FALL/mp02.html)
Joined 15 years of Census ACS and BLS QCEW data across **350+ metro areas** to construct a rent-burden index and housing-growth score, identifying 108 "YIMBY success" metros and quantifying their labor-market impact in a congressional-style policy brief.
`R` · `Census ACS` · `BLS QCEW` · `Index Construction` · `Policy Writing`

### 🌳 [NYC Urban Forestry: Tree Canopy & Policy Proposal](https://paven227.github.io/STA9750-2025-FALL/mp03.html)
Spatially joined **683,788 NYC street trees** to City Council districts to quantify tree health disparities by borough, then translated the findings into a funded ($525K) policy proposal for the highest-need district, backed by comparative district-level statistics.
`R` · `sf` (Spatial) · `Leaflet` · `NYC Open Data` · `Geospatial Analysis`

### 📰 [Fact-Checking BLS Jobs Data: A Statistical Investigation](https://paven227.github.io/STA9750-2025-FALL/mp04.html)
Applied bootstrap and permutation testing to **46 years of BLS employment data** (1979–2025) to evaluate political claims of "rigged" jobs revisions, finding no statistical evidence of bias and returning verdicts backed by hypothesis tests rather than assertion.
`R` · `Hypothesis Testing` · `Bootstrap/Permutation Methods` · `infer`

### 🚦 [NYC Traffic & Road Degradation Data Warehouse](https://github.com/PAVEN227/baruch-cis-9440-dbt)
Co-built a Kimball-style dimensional warehouse in BigQuery using dbt, modeling NYC traffic and road-condition data into fact and dimension tables to support downstream reporting and analysis.
`BigQuery` · `dbt` · `SQL` · `Dimensional Modeling`
